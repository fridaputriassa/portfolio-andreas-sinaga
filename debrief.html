// context

Where the internship sat

About the ITFC Division

The IT Financial Control (ITFC) Division is responsible for managing and controlling Bank Danamon's IT financial data and IT assets, to ensure accuracy, security, efficiency, and compliance with internal policies and regulatory requirements.

My role within ITFC

I worked with the ITFC Asset team, which manages IT assets, inventory documentation, and asset-related processes. My focus was asset recording, JIRA ticket handling, delivery, stock opname, auction validation, and building a digital inventory tool.

01Ensure accuracy and reliability of IT financial data.
02Manage and maintain IT assets and inventory.
03Support budgeting, reporting, and cost control.
04Ensure compliance with internal policies and regulations.

// 04 · stock opname

Hardware and software stock opname

HARDWARE STOCK OPNAME

  • Assisted hardware stock opname at the BSD Asset Warehouse.
  • Checked and verified about 300 IT assets handed over to ITFC Asset.
  • Validated serial numbers and asset data against the inventory list.
  • Made sure asset information matched the physical devices.

SOFTWARE STOCK OPNAME

  • Created master data for the software stock opname activity.
  • Conducted software stock opname with two user areas.
  • Validated software inventory data with the related users.
  • Prepared BA validation documents once the stock opname was complete.
